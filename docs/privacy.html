Privacy Policy

Application: Expenses to Spreadsheet · Last updated: 27 July 2026

Expenses to Spreadsheet is a personal, non-commercial application operated by its author solely to access the author's own bank account(s) and organise the author's own expenses. It is not offered as a service to any other person.

1. Data controller & contact

The application is operated by Leonardo Baroncelli, who is also the sole user and the only data subject. For any data-protection matter, contact: leonardo.baroncelli26@gmail.com.

2. What data is accessed

Through Enable Banking (a licensed account-information service provider), the application reads transaction data from the account holder's own bank account: transaction amount, currency, booking date, description, and a transaction identifier. It does not initiate payments and does not access credentials — authentication happens directly with the bank.

3. Why it is processed

Solely to categorise the account holder's own expenses and record them in the account holder's own spreadsheet, for personal budgeting. The legal basis is the account holder's own consent, given each time the bank connection is authorised.

4. Where data is stored

  • Categorised expenses are written to a Google Spreadsheet owned by the account holder.
  • Identifiers of already-processed transactions are stored locally to avoid duplicates.
  • Optional notifications are sent to the account holder's own Telegram chat.

No data is sold, shared with third parties for their own purposes, or used for advertising or analytics.

5. Third-party processors

The application relies on: Enable Banking (open-banking account access), Google (Sheets storage), and Telegram (notifications). Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Retention & deletion

Data is retained only while the account holder chooses to run the application. The account holder can delete the local state file and the spreadsheet contents, and revoke the bank consent, at any time.

7. Your rights

As the sole data subject and operator, the account holder may access, correct, export or erase all data at will. Bank access can be withdrawn at any time via the bank or by letting the consent expire.
